# Plan 4: settle triggers, templates, and language styles

**Status: draft.** The owner accepted the mechanics of the stock news in [Plan 6](06-trigger-rules.md). This stage uses offline replay to settle what the newspaper covers and how it sounds. [Plan 7](07-llm-editors.md) will use the resulting catalogue and style guidance.

## Goal and success criteria

Settle a useful stock mix of city, national, and foreign news. Triggers should fire at a meaningful moment, group related developments, and leave quiet periods quiet. Templates should turn those developments into readable stories with distinct voices suited to the publication's medium and the evidence. Review a representative set of recorded issues with the owner and save the accepted catalogue and examples as the heuristic baseline for Plan 7.

## Current state

The [trigger guide](../../../developers/vox-agents/newspaper/triggers/overview.md) documents the 14 stock rules and their audits. The matching templates already vary tone for local settler departures and wartime Golden Ages. `editorial/config/{load,schema}.ts` loads and validates the catalogue, including overrides; `editorial/selection/templates.ts` renders eligible variants. None of these exposes shared language guidance yet. [Replay](../../../developers/vox-agents/newspaper/replay.md) already stores issues, votes, series history and audits and can regenerate issues from a recording.

## Ordered work

1. **Settle the trigger mix.** Replay the [stock reading examples](../../../developers/vox-agents/newspaper/replay.md#stock-catalogue-reading-examples), then sample other receivers and periods where the evidence supports a comparison. Use the audits to tune thresholds, importance, urgency, grouping, and repeat rules. Adjust decay, novelty, vote effects, and page minimums where the reading shows a problem. Keep rule IDs stable where the meaning is unchanged. For example, read the Dutch research stories at turn 59 together and decide whether they deserve separate articles or one roundup. Distinguish a missing development from a candidate that lost its place on the page. Add coverage where the recorded evidence can support it.
2. **Define shared language styles.** Add `language-styles.json` under `vox-agents/newspaper/` and load it through `editorial/config/{load,schema}.ts`. Give each English style a stable ID and short guidance with examples for headlines, ledes, diction, length, and uncertainty. Use a general news style as the default across media. Style is the voice of the medium, such as a settlement account or a gazette. Stance is the treatment of a particular development, such as supportive, neutral, or critical. Either medium can carry any supported stance. Base the style set on the [spec's media and voice](../spec.md#5-evolution-with-technology) and review it for readability, local character, and restraint.
3. **Revise authored templates.** Attach a validated style ID to each variant, with the general style as the default. Variant conditions and weights establish when that voice and stance apply. Use reportable research facts when available and the recorded era otherwise. Design and prose can use the same era or research facts and evolve at different rates; the choice of visual design does not filter variants. Hand Plan 7 the resolved guidance for the variants eligible on each candidate, with the general style available for angles without a matching variant. Keep claims and links grounded in evidence. For example, damage and dwindling stores support a story on shortages; naming an attacker requires evidence that identifies one. Add an optional style map beside `selectedVariants` in `contracts/publication.ts` for the decision audit. Save the style catalogue in the run configuration and package it in `scripts/installer.iss`. Older saved configurations default to the general style; existing issues retain their stored copy.
4. **Record the baseline.** Save the recording identifiers, receivers, turn ranges, and effective configuration needed to regenerate the accepted samples. For cases with votes, record the cutoff and article to vote on using the existing replay controls. Include useful short pieces, quiet checks, and a continuing, merged, and returning series where available. Document missing cases and label synthetic supplements or era approximations. Update the [evaluation guide](../../../developers/vox-agents/newspaper/evaluation.md) with the reading set and the [editorial guide](../../../developers/vox-agents/newspaper/editorial.md) with the style handoff to Plan 7. Refresh trigger and replay examples affected by the new catalogue.

## Separate reading workflow

Finish bounded archive filters for date, page, civilization, and city and support series links to the archived page and scroll target. Extend the replay store and browser shell with the shared archive contracts in `mcp-server/src/newspaper/protocol.ts` and Vox Agents' `contracts/archive.ts`. Keep the archive and evidence inspector outside the publication and preserve shared votes across page appearances. Exercise ordinary, breaking, and analysis layouts with the current designs; decide whether extra visual designs are needed once Plan 1's broader renderer and size checks are complete. The editorial baseline can be accepted using the existing replay controls.

## Verification and scope

Compare fresh stock and tuned replay runs against the same inputs. Inspect the recorded articles and their audits for selection, repetition, support for claims, and consistency between the prose and the chosen style. Verify that missing facts lead to a supported neutral variant or a skipped story, that saved issues retain their prose and links, and that the packaged catalogue loads with its style references intact. Run `npm run build -w vox-agents` and `npm run test:mock -w vox-agents -- tests/mock/newspaper`. Check representative previews and votes in the browser; exercise archive filters and series links when that work unit lands.

The owner accepts the recorded issues for usefulness, story mix, continuity, and voice. Any remaining broader in-game renderer checks stay in [Plan 1](01-renderer.md). Model calls and comparisons between editors belong to [Plan 7](07-llm-editors.md). Translation and live publication are outside this stage.
