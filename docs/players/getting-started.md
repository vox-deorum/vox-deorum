# Getting Started

Vox Deorum lets you play Civilization V against opponents powered by language models such as GPT, Claude, and Gemini. They make strategic decisions and talk to you in character.

## What you need

- Windows 10 or 11.
- [Civilization V](https://store.steampowered.com/app/8930/) installed through Steam, ideally with both expansions: _Gods & Kings_ and _Brave New World_. Vox Deorum builds on [Community Patch and Vox Populi](https://github.com/LoneGazebo/Community-Patch-DLL) and is tested with the full game.
- An AI service account or a local model. Hosted providers may charge for usage; see [Configuration](configuration.md).

## Install

1. Download the installer from the [releases page](https://github.com/CIVITAS-John/vox-deorum/releases).
2. Run it and confirm your Civilization V folder when asked. The installer sets up the mods and AI services for you.

If the installer cannot find your game, see [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md).

## First launch

Start **Vox Deorum** from the Start Menu. It opens a console window and the dashboard in your browser. Keep the console open while playing and use its prompt to stop when you finish. If the dashboard does not open, visit `http://localhost:5555`.

Follow Setup to connect an AI service and choose your Main AI. Keep the optional Judge AI off for your first game.

Then open **Play** and follow the game setup. You can play normally, watch the AIs play, or direct a civilization's strategy while the game handles its units and cities. Start with the default settings; Vox Deorum launches Civilization V with the mods enabled.
